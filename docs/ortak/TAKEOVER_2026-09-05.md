## Immediate security gate: B1b

The normalizer's reads are snapshots outside the routing transaction. Membership,
bindings, channel state/visibility, authors, parents and publish-root provenance
must be authoritative at routing commit and queued-run admission.

The root lock or a READ COMMITTED reread alone does not serialize Office writers.
Select a coordinated mutation/row-range/version-fencing design covering existing
and absent rows. The retained schema's community-deletion fence requires READ
COMMITTED; do not blindly switch to SERIALIZABLE. Global SHARE table locks were
discussed only as a costly option, not accepted architecture. Resolve lock order,
phantoms, runtime-admission linearization and post-admission cancellation; never
hold a DB transaction over a runtime network call.

Test adversarial mutations before and after the authoritative reread, not only
the easy removal-before-commit case. See B1 §8 for the complete checklist.
